Intercept · α̂
Slope · β̂
Squared errors · SSR

OLS chooses the intercept and slope that minimise the sum of squared vertical residuals.

SSR = Σ(yᵢ − ŷᵢ)²
Units here: percentage points squared.

01 / THE LINE

Prediction, not certainty

ŷ = α̂ + β̂X. The intercept predicts stock return when market return is 0%. The slope gives the predicted change in stock return for a 1 percentage point rise in market return.

02 / THE GAPS

Actual minus predicted

A residual is eᵢ = yᵢ − ŷᵢ. A point above the line has a positive residual; below the line, a negative one. The gaps are vertical because we predict Y from X.

03 / THE SQUARES

Big errors count more

Squaring stops positive and negative errors cancelling. A gap of 4 contributes 16 to SSR; a gap of 2 contributes 4. Move a dot far away and watch the fitted line respond.

Exam checkpoint

OLS estimates regression coefficients by minimising the sum of squared residuals. R² describes the share of sample variation in Y explained by this fitted regression (with an intercept). A good fit alone does not prove causation, unbiased estimates, or valid t-tests. Assumptions about errors and the data matter.
